Student notes

Notes for fellow students

I kept losing track of this stuff, so I put it in one place. No obvious stuff. Only the less obvious, K-State specific, time sensitive, or easy to miss ones.

Last updated: Oct 15, 2026 · Feel free to share · Send corrections if something expired.

Quick rules: Check the official page before adding a card. Most free trials auto-renew. Set a reminder on the day you claim anything. Not affiliated, not sponsored. Use @k-state.edu, not @ksu.edu, when a K-State service asks for your email.

K-StateFree

Libraries + Interlibrary Loan

Never pay for a paper first. Check the library, then ILL.

Set Scholar to K-State, install LibKey Nomad. Most ILL articles arrive as PDFs, often within a day.
