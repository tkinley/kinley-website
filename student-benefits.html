Skip these

  • Cursor free student Pro: expired June 2026.
  • Perplexity free Pro: ended. Now $10/mo Education Pro.
  • Todoist student discount: does not exist.
  • Canva for Education: K-12 only for college students.
  • Claude student codes: no such code exists.
  • Grammarly Pro free with .edu: false.
  • Adobe free for students: false. Discounted only, and at K-State free Adobe is faculty / staff only.
  • Cerebras free tier: conflicting. Earlier said gone, newer secondary lists claim 1M tokens per day free. Verify on Cerebras official pages.
  • Fly.io: no free tier.
  • Render free Postgres: 30 days only.
  • Together AI: no free tier per one 2026 tracker, conflicting.
  • Supermaven free tier: unconfirmed.
  • Freevee: shut down in 2025.
  • Kansas State Parks pass: 2022 offer only, do not claim.
  • FEMS Early Career award: closed Sep 30, 2026.
  • Fulbright 2026: national deadline Oct 6, 2026 passed.
  • MSA Feb 1, 2026: passed.
  • Warby Parker student discount: no verified programme, conflicting claims.
  • Zipcar at K-State: no partnership found.
  • Tastecard: UK only.
  • MongoDB University US applicability: unverified.
  • Tabnine free tier: gone in 2026.
  • Mendeley Reference Manager: Elsevier stripped out local folders and locked down syncing. Use Zotero instead.
  • SnapGene "student discounts": Still expensive and restrictive; use Benchling (academic) or Ape / SnapGene Viewer for free.
  • "Free" GitHub Pack domains (.me / .tech / Namecheap) in Year 2: Free for 12 months, then auto-renews at $30–$60/yr if you leave a card on file.
  • ResearchGate "Request Full-Text" button: Authors rarely check it. K-State ILL or LibKey Nomad is almost always faster.
  • Overleaf paid student plan for group papers: Free tier hits compile timeouts on big theses. Use local VS Code + LaTeX Workshop + Git instead.
