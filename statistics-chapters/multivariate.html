Centering, scaling, and distance

Centering subtracts a feature mean. Standardization also divides by its standard deviation. A distance-based analysis depends on these choices because coordinates with a larger numerical range can dominate distances.

Intuition

Combining measurements in thousands and tenths puts them on different numerical rulers. Standardization uses each feature’s typical variation as the ruler.

Worked math

  1. Two samples differ by 100 on feature 1 and by 1 on feature 2. Their raw Euclidean distance is √(100² + 1²) ≈ 100.005.
  2. If feature SDs are 100 and 0.5, differences in SD units are 1 and 2.
  3. Their standardized Euclidean distance is √(1² + 2²) = √5. The second feature now contributes more.

In bioinformatics

Choose a scale that reflects meaningful variation across samples. Standardizing every noisy low-variance feature can also amplify measurement noise.

Covariance and correlation

A covariance matrix stores variances on its diagonal and pairwise covariances off the diagonal. Correlation divides covariance by both feature SDs, yielding a unitless measure of linear association. These matrices summarize second-order structure.

Intuition

A positive off-diagonal value means samples high on one centered feature tend to be high on the other. It can reflect shared biology, a technical factor, or both.

Worked math

  1. If Var(X) = Var(Y) = 4 and Cov(X,Y) = 3, then each SD is 2.
  2. The correlation is 3/(2 × 2) = 0.75.
  3. Changing X’s units multiplies its SD and covariance with Y by the same positive factor, leaving correlation unchanged.

In bioinformatics

Examine whether strong feature correlations persist after accounting for known batch and sample structure.

Principal components, scores, and loadings

PCA finds orthogonal directions of decreasing variance in centered data. Loadings give the direction’s coefficients for each feature. A score is a sample’s coordinate along that direction. Eigenvalues measure variance along the corresponding directions.

Intuition

Rotate the axes until the first axis follows the widest direction of the point cloud. The second axis captures the remaining variation perpendicular to it.

Worked math

  1. For covariance [[4,3],[3,4]], the sum direction (1,1)/√2 has eigenvalue 7 and the difference direction (1,−1)/√2 has eigenvalue 1.
  2. The first component explains 7/(7+1) = 87.5% of variance.
  3. A centered sample at (2,2) scores 2√2 on PC1 and zero on PC2. Changing the sign of the loadings changes the sign of scores without changing the geometry.

In bioinformatics

Color a PCA plot by condition, batch, or quality metrics to explore structure. High variance along a component does not establish what caused it.

Clustering and linkage

Clustering groups samples using a chosen representation and similarity rule. In hierarchical clustering, the distance between clusters depends on the linkage rule. Single linkage uses the nearest pair, while complete linkage uses the farthest pair.

Intuition

Single linkage can connect a chain of nearby points into a long cluster. Complete linkage favors groups whose members stay close even at the extremes.

Worked math

  1. Let d(A,B)=2, d(A,C)=4, and d(B,C)=5. A and B merge first.
  2. The single-linkage distance from {A,B} to C is min(4,5)=4.
  3. The complete-linkage distance is max(4,5)=5. The same input can produce different dendrogram heights.

In bioinformatics

Check whether patterns persist under reasonable choices of distance, transformation, and linkage before naming clusters.

Rank and high-dimensional noise

When the number of features p exceeds the sample count n, centered data have rank at most n−1. A sample covariance matrix then has many directions with zero estimated variance and may be singular. Feature selection and regularization become important.

Intuition

Measuring more coordinates gives more ways to describe the same small set of points. It does not add independent samples.

Worked math

  1. With n = 20 samples and p = 1,000 features, the centered matrix has rank at most 19.
  2. At most 19 PCA components can have nonzero sample variance.
  3. A model that inverts the full 1,000-by-1,000 sample covariance needs additional structure or regularization.

In bioinformatics

Use independent samples to judge whether a high-dimensional pattern generalizes. Apparent separation in a visualization can arise from selected features.

Practice questions

1. Interpret a principal component

A covariance matrix has eigenvalues 7 and 1. How much variance does the first component explain, and what does that tell you?

Show the answer

7/8 = 87.5%. The first direction captures that share of the variance in the sampled, preprocessed data. It does not establish causation.

2. Count possible nonzero components

You center a matrix with 20 samples and 1,000 features. What is the maximum number of nonzero-variance principal components?

Show the answer

At most 19. The centered matrix has rank no larger than min(20−1, 1000).
