    How this works

    Checked facts. The big funding entries were checked on their official pages. Each card says when. Everything else comes from my resource list and may be out of date: those cards say “Not re-checked yet,” and anything in my notes is quoted as notes, not facts.

    Ruled out means a checked rule. A card moves to “Not for you” only when a rule read from its official page excludes your level or citizenship. Rules that come from my own notes and haven't been checked show as a warning on the card instead, and those cards sort last.

    Private. Matching runs in your browser. No uploads or cookies: only anonymous visit counts (GoatCounter), and no IP addresses are stored. Level, needs, and the K-State toggle go in the address bar so you can bookmark the list; citizenship never does.
