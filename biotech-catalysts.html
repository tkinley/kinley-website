The Feb–Mar 2027 PDUFA cluster is the centre of gravity

Every public catalyst calendar checked (MerlinTrader, BioPharmaWatch, BioRadar, the 2027 Biotech Catalyst Calendar) leads its H1 2027 regulatory coverage with the same dense run: BBIO Feb 4 → BLTE Feb 12 → PVLA / SRPT Feb 28 → EXEL Mar 3 → NTLA Mar 10 → AQST Mar 16. Two of this list’s top five sit inside that single six-week stretch.

Other names calendars collectively flag outside the top 8: SRPT AMONDYS / VYONDYS traditional-approval conversion (Feb 28, 2027), EXEL zanzalintinib + atezolizumab (Mar 3, 2027, after a major-amendment extension), CGEM zipalertinib (Feb 27, 2027, 89% PoA), and NBIX’s two H1 / Q1 2027 Phase 3 readouts (osavampator in MDD, dereclidine in schizophrenia) — the last is the direct competitive read-across for LBRX.

Weekly monitoring checklist

What to check each week, per name — specifically, what news would change the thesis before the date arrives. No AdCom is currently scheduled for any name here; for BLTE, PVLA, and NTLA, an AdCom scheduling notice would itself be a tradable event.

Each Monday

Scan FDA and company press releases for the two nearest dates on the timeline. AdCom scheduling, a major-amendment extension, or a ClinicalTrials.gov date move are the three highest-signal items.

Mid-week

Check the thesis-breakers, not the price: NTLA liver-safety disclosures in either CRISPR program, VKTX primary-completion estimate, LBRX enrollment-completion news (it starts the 6-week readout clock).

Before each date

Re-read the card’s risk list and the sequencing warnings — PRAX moves on relutrigine (Dec 27, 2026) and COGT on GIST / NonAdvSM (Nov 30 / Dec 30, 2026) well before their 2027 dates.
